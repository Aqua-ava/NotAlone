Copy a file → Paste it into your AI → Start asking for help

## Step 2: Copy the SKILL.md File 📋

The magic is in a single file called **SKILL.md**. It contains all the instructions that transform a generic AI into your dating coach.

### How to Get It

1. **Go to the EROS repository** on GitHub
2. **Find the file called `SKILL.md`** in the root folder (it's the main file — you can't miss it)
3. **Click on the file** to open it
4. **Copy the entire contents:**
   - Click the "Raw" button (top-right of the file viewer) to see the plain text
   - Press `Ctrl+A` (or `Cmd+A` on Mac) to select everything
   - Press `Ctrl+C` (or `Cmd+C` on Mac) to copy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Copy the ENTIRE file.** Don't skip sections or try to trim it down. Every part works together — the profile optimization, conversation coaching, psychology decoding, date planning, ethics guardrails — it's all one integrated system.

### What's In This File?

You don't need to read it all (your AI will), but here's what you're giving your AI:

- 🎯 **Profile Optimization** — How to audit and improve dating profiles
- 💬 **Conversation Mastery** — Opening messages, banter, reading signals
- 📅 **Date Planning** — Creating memorable, low-pressure experiences
- 🧠 **Psychology Decoding** — Understanding what messages really mean
- 🛡️ **Confidence Building** — Reframing rejection, building resilience
- ❤️ **Relationship Advice** — Communication, conflict, keeping the spark
- 🤝 **Ethics** — Hard guardrails against manipulation or deception

## Step 3: Paste It Into a New Conversation 💬

Now for the actual setup:

1. **Open your AI chatbot** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.)
2. **Start a brand new conversation** (don't paste it into an existing one)
3. **Paste the entire SKILL.md content** into the message box
4. **Hit Send**

Your AI will read the instructions and transform into EROS. You'll typically see a greeting like:

> *"I'm EROS, your AI Dating Intelligence Assistant. How can I help with your dating life today?"*

That means it worked. You're ready to go. 🎉

> [!TIP]
> **Pro move:** Some AI platforms (like ChatGPT and Claude) let you create "Custom Instructions" or "Projects" where you can paste the SKILL.md once and it stays active across all future conversations. This means you don't have to re-paste it every time. Look for settings like:
> - **ChatGPT:** "Customize ChatGPT" → Custom Instructions
> - **Claude:** "Projects" → Create a new project → Add to Project Knowledge
> - **Gemini:** "Gems" → Create a custom Gem

## Step 4: Start Using EROS 🚀

Now the fun part. EROS comes with **11 quick commands** you can use right away. Just type the command and follow up with whatever context EROS asks for.

### The Quick Commands

| Command | What It Does | Try Saying... |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| `/profile-review` | Reviews your dating profile with specific, actionable feedback | *"/profile-review — here's my Hinge profile: [paste your bio and describe your photos]"* |
| `/write-bio` | Creates 3 custom bio options based on your personality | *"/write-bio — I'm into rock climbing, bad puns, and I make a mean breakfast burrito"* |
| `/opener` | Crafts 3 opening messages for someone you matched with | *"/opener — her bio says she loves hiking and her dog is named Biscuit"* |
| `/reply` | Analyzes their message and gives you 2-3 response options | *"/reply — she said: 'haha that's funny. so what do you do?'"* |
| `/date-idea` | Suggests 3 personalized date ideas | *"/date-idea — she's artsy, loves wine, and we're in Chicago"* |
| `/decode` | Decodes what their message *really* means | *"/decode — she said 'I'm busy this week' after we'd been texting for 3 days"* |
| `/crisis` | Helps you handle a dating crisis with grace | *"/crisis — I got ghosted after what I thought was an amazing first date"* |
| `/confidence` | Delivers a personalized confidence boost | *"/confidence — I've been on 5 dates this month and none went anywhere"* |
| `/red-flag-check` | Analyzes behaviors for potential red flags | *"/red-flag-check — he got upset when I said I was hanging out with my guy friend"* |
| `/vibe-check` | Reviews your drafted message before you send it | *"/vibe-check — I want to send: 'Hey beautiful, I'd love to take you out sometime'"* |
| `/relationship` | Advice for existing relationships | *"/relationship — my partner and I have been arguing about the same thing for weeks"* |

### Your First Conversation — Try One of These

Not sure where to start? Here are three great first interactions:

**If you're building a profile:**
> *"Hey EROS, I'm setting up a new Hinge profile. Can you help me write a bio? I'm a 28-year-old software engineer who loves cooking, hiking, and terrible horror movies. My friends would describe me as funny but kind of a nerd. I want to attract someone who's smart, doesn't take themselves too seriously, and likes trying new restaurants."*

**If you need help with a conversation:**
> *"I matched with someone and her bio says she's a veterinarian who loves Thai food and true crime podcasts. Can you help me write an opening message? I want something playful, not generic."*

**If you're navigating a tricky situation:**
> *"I've been texting this girl for a week and the conversation is going great, but I'm nervous about asking her out. Can you help me figure out how and when to transition to suggesting we meet up?"*

## 🔥 Tips for Getting the Best Results

### Give Context

The more EROS knows about your situation, the better the advice. Instead of:

> ❌ *"Help me write a message"*

Try:

> ✅ *"I've been texting this woman for 3 days. We matched on Hinge. She's really into yoga and travel. Our vibe has been playful and flirty. She just sent me: 'so when are you gonna ask me out? 😏' — help me reply!"*

### Be Honest About Your Style

EROS adapts to *you*. Let it know:

- **Your personality** — "I'm more on the introverted side" or "I'm pretty outgoing"
- **Your humor style** — "I love dry humor" or "I'm more of a wholesome funny person"
- **Your experience level** — "I'm new to dating apps" or "I've been at this for a while"
- **What you're looking for** — "Something serious" or "Keeping it casual for now"

### Pick the Option That Feels Like *You*

EROS usually gives you 2-3 response options ranging from safe to bold. **Always pick the one that sounds most like something you'd actually say.** The goal isn't to be someone you're not — it's to be the best, most articulate version of who you already are.

### Use It as a Coach, Not a Crutch

The whole point of EROS is to help you **learn**. Pay attention to *why* certain messages work. Notice the patterns. Over time, you'll internalize the principles and won't need to check in as often. That's the goal.

### Have an Ongoing Conversation

EROS gets better the longer you talk to it. It learns your situation, your personality, your history with someone. Don't just fire off one-off questions — have an ongoing conversation:

> *"Remember that woman I asked you about yesterday? She responded! Here's what she said..."*

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### "Does this work with the free version of ChatGPT?"

**Yes, absolutely.** EROS works great with free ChatGPT (GPT-3.5), free Claude, free Gemini — all of them. Paid versions give you longer conversations and slightly better analysis, but the free tier is more than enough to get started.

### "Can I use this on my phone?"

**Yes!** ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all have mobile apps (iOS and Android). You can paste the SKILL.md into any of them. This means you can get real-time advice while you're actively texting someone — which is incredibly useful.

### "Do I have to paste the whole thing every time?"

**Not necessarily.** Here are your options:

1. **Paste once per conversation** — Start a new chat, paste SKILL.md, and keep that conversation going for as long as you need
2. **Use Custom Instructions/Projects** — Most AI platforms let you save instructions permanently (see the pro tip in Step 3)
3. **Bookmark the raw file** — Keep the GitHub raw file URL bookmarked for quick copy-paste access

### "Is this... cheating?"

**No.** And we address this honestly in our [Ethical Framework](ethical-framework.md). Here's the short version: You use spellcheck when you write emails. You rehearse job interviews. You ask friends for advice on what to wear on dates. EROS is the same thing — it helps you *communicate* what you already feel. It doesn't create fake feelings or misrepresent who you are.

### "Will the other person know I'm using AI?"

**Only if you tell them.** EROS doesn't add watermarks or signatures. The messages it helps you craft are meant to sound like *you* — because they're based on *your* personality and *your* situation. That said, never copy-paste AI text word-for-word without putting it in your own voice. Use EROS as a starting point, then adjust to sound like yourself.

### "What if the AI gives me bad advice?"

**Use your judgment.** EROS is a tool, not a substitute for your own instincts. If something doesn't feel right, don't do it. If a suggested message doesn't sound like you, don't send it. The AI doesn't know the other person the way you do — it's working from whatever context you provide. The more context you give, the better the advice.

### "Can I use this if I'm a woman?"

**Yes!** EROS is designed for everyone, regardless of gender or orientation. The principles of authentic communication, confidence, and emotional intelligence are universal. Some of the example scenarios use he/she language, but the advice applies to all humans trying to connect with other humans.

### "I'm not looking for anything serious. Does this still work?"

**Yes.** Whether you're looking for a long-term relationship, casual dating, or just want to get better at meeting people, EROS adapts to your goals. Just tell it what you're looking for and the advice will calibrate accordingly.

### "Can EROS help with in-person social situations too?"

**Definitely.** While it's primarily designed for dating apps and texting, the conversation principles, confidence frameworks, and psychology insights work just as well for in-person interactions. Try:

> *"I'm going to a bar tonight and I tend to freeze up when I see someone attractive. Can you give me some natural conversation starters and help me think about how to approach someone?"*

### "What if I'm neurodivergent and social cues are hard for me?"

**EROS is especially useful for you.** The `/decode` command literally breaks down messages into three layers (surface, strategic, emotional) — which is exactly the kind of explicit social decoding that can be incredibly helpful for people who find subtext challenging. Don't hesitate to tell EROS about your communication style so it can tailor its advice.
