## 🧪 How It Works

EROS is based on actual psychology research — not pickup artist nonsense. Here's the science behind it:

### 🧬 The Psychology Stack

| Layer | Science | What It Does For You |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **Personality Reading** | Big Five / OCEAN Model | Understand what kind of person she is from how she texts |
| **Communication Theory** | Hyperpersonal Model, Pragmatics | Write messages that create real emotional connection |
| **Humor Science** | Benign Violation Theory | Be funny without being try-hard or offensive |
| **Behavioral Economics** | Loss Aversion, Variable Rewards | Create healthy intrigue without playing games |
| **Neurobiology** | Dopamine/Oxytocin Pathways | Understand why attraction works the way it does |
| **Attachment Theory** | Bowlby & Ainsworth | Navigate different attachment styles with empathy |
| **Environmental Psychology** | Mehrabian's Research | Design dates that feel naturally intimate |

## 🤝 The Ethics

We believe strongly in ethical AI-assisted dating. EROS is built on these principles:

1. **🪞 The Mirror, Not The Mask** — EROS helps you show who you really are, better. It doesn't create a fake version of you.

2. **🎓 Coach, Not Puppet** — EROS explains *why* something works so you learn and grow. The goal is to eventually not need it.

3. **🚫 Never Manipulate** — No negging, no emotional manipulation, no deception. If a strategy would make you feel ashamed when the truth comes out, EROS won't recommend it.

4. **✊ Respect Always** — Every person on the other end of that screen is a real human with real feelings. Period.

5. **🔐 Privacy First** — Everything stays between you and your AI. We don't collect data, track usage, or sell anything.

> *"Using AI for dating isn't cheating — it's the same as asking a friend for advice, rehearsing what to say before a presentation, or drafting an important email. You're not changing who you are. You're removing the barrier between what you feel and how you communicate it."*

Read the full ethical framework → [`docs/ethical-framework.md`](docs/ethical-framework.md)

## 🌟 Examples

### Profile Before & After

**Before:**
> "I like hiking, Netflix, and tacos. Looking for my partner in crime. Please don't be boring. 6'1 because apparently that matters."

**After (EROS-optimized):**
> "I'll plan the best spontaneous date you've ever been on — but I'll still burn the garlic bread every time. Currently training for a half marathon and losing an argument with a sourdough starter. Tell me your most controversial food take. I need to know before this goes further."

### Message Analysis

**Her text:** *"I'm busy this week but maybe next week?"*

**EROS Analysis:**
| Layer | Reading |
|-------|---------|
| Surface | She can't meet this week, suggests next week |
| Strategic | The "maybe" adds uncertainty — she's interested but not fully committed |
| Emotional | She wants to keep the door open but isn't ready to lock in plans |

**Suggested response:** *"No rush! Just let me know when your schedule opens up. I'll be over here perfecting my terrible pasta recipe."*

**Why it works:** Shows patience (no neediness), humor (self-deprecation), and leaves the ball in her court without pressure.
